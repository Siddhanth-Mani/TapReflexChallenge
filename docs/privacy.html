Tap Reflex — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tap Reflex ("the game") is a reflex game for Android made by Siddhanth Mani in Fiji. This page explains what the game does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your phone.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What is saved on your phone

Your progress (scores, level, rank, badges, chosen skin and settings) is saved on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. It never leaves your phone through the game.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup in your phone's settings, Android may include this saved progress in your Google account backup, so it comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone. That backup is run by Android and Google under your settings and their privacy policy, not by the game. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations when you hit or miss a target. You can turn this off in the game's settings.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes all saved progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
